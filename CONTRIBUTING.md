# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in Street Fighter II CPS1 to SNES. This independent, non-commercial project aims to reproduce the original CPS1 experience on standard SNES hardware without enhancement chips.

## Public contributions

Useful contributions include English documentation improvements, corrections to public statements, clearly described gameplay observations, and suggestions about player-facing behavior. Before starting substantial work, open an issue to discuss its scope and avoid duplicate effort.

Keep discussion respectful, specific, and focused on observable behavior. Distinguish suggestions and expectations from verified results.

## Reporting an observation

When applicable, include:

- A concise summary and the expected behavior.
- Steps to reproduce the observation and what actually happened.
- The revision or explicitly announced release involved, if known.
- The test environment, including whether the observation came from standard SNES hardware or an emulator.
- Any limits on reproducibility or verification.

Do not imply access to a public build unless an official project release announcement confirms it. If you cannot verify a detail, say so.

## Documentation pull requests

1. Work on a separate branch and keep the change focused.
2. Write in clear English and check relative links and Markdown formatting.
3. Support status claims with evidence and preserve the distinction between goals, observations, and confirmed results.
4. Update the changelog when the change affects public project information.
5. Open a pull request describing the change and how you checked it. Wait for maintainer review before merging.

Documentation contributions do not authorize publication of a build or release. Follow the [Release Policy](docs/RELEASE_POLICY.md) for release decisions.

## Public content boundaries

Do not submit source code, internal tools, memory management details, conversion techniques, ROM files, patches, or copyrighted assets in public contributions, issue attachments, or pull requests. Do not provide links to unauthorized copies of third-party material. Use original written descriptions of observations instead.

Only contribute text you have the right to share. Non-commercial intent does not remove third-party rights. Do not add licensing claims on behalf of the project or its rights holders.
